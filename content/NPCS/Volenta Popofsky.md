![[Pasted image 20261014001141.png|300]]

A short woman the party met in the town square of [[Vallaki]], who lingered after the Baron's petitions with such a carefree air that she seemed to float on it. She wears a shoulderless gold dress, large brown boots, and a mask made from the skull of a horned creature.

She was fascinated by [[Virro]]'s ears, and touched them in amazement once [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] explained they were real. She has little love for the Baron, and offered the party "a bit of... _revolutionary violence_". When Ignacious wondered whether the Baron might be a vampire, she laughed: "Oh, no. I remember the day he became Baron." Asked her age, she held up a bare ring finger. "Careful, I'm married!" A pause. "Well, he is dead, though."

In a side alley she kissed [[Retnuh]], catching him completely off guard. She mentioned that the [[Keepers of the Feather]] might be willing to stir things up in town, though she had no idea where to find them.

At the [[Blue Water Inn]] she drank a bottle of Red Strongwash straight from the neck. When [[Karl Wachter]] belched in Retnuh's ear and Retnuh punched him, Volenta broke the bottle over Karl's head and kicked him unconscious.

[[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] isn't sure she can be trusted, but is fairly certain she isn't a vampire: the wards on Vallaki keep them out.

As the brawl at the inn spread, several of the quieter drinkers stepped in without a word and pinned her arms. The moment she was released, she vanished through the door.
