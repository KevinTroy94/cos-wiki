One of three Vistani women at the [[The Blood of the Vine]] tavern in Barovia village, alongside [[Sorvia]] and [[Alenka]]. A playful brunette, quick with a smirk and quicker with a comment, clearly the one who steered conversation wherever she wanted it to go.

When [[Virro]] tried to press her for information, she shut him down and turned it into a drinking game instead. When he later tried to alter the tab sheet while [[Arik]] had stepped away, she caught him, told him it was brazen, wrote his name on the tabs, and threw them in the fire.

She and her companions own the tavern.
