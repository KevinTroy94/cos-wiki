[[Vallaki]]'s toymaker, proprietor of [[Blinsky's Toys]]. A warm, energetic man who bounces on his heels with every word, with a fondness for eerie toys and a thick accent. "Ah, wyelcome, wyelcome, friends! Come, come, see the leetle wonders of Blinsky!" At his side perches Piccolo, a white-faced capuchin monkey in a tutu. His slogan: "Is no fun, is no Blinsky!"

[[Virro]] bought a hand puppet of Strahd from him. On a high shelf, [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] found a doll made in her exact likeness. Blinsky, delighted at the resemblance, admitted the dolls were [[Izek Strazni]]'s doing: each month Izek demands a new one in lieu of taxes. Blinsky makes them and hands them over, and has no idea what they are for.

> [!dmspoiler]
> "Vallaki's toymaker, Gadof Blinsky (CG male human **commoner**), calls himself "a wizard of tiny wonders," but he has been consumed by despair lately because no one seems to like him or want his toys. His fascination for eerie playthings causes most other locals to avoid him. The burgomaster enables Blinsky to stay in business by giving him a couple of gold pieces a month to make festival decorations."
>
> "In the past six months, the only paying customer who has set foot in the store is a visitor from a faraway land named Rictavio, who came in two weeks ago and bought a stuffed Vistana doll. Realizing that the toymaker was lonely, Rictavio gave Blinsky his pet monkey, Piccolo."
>
> Izek keeps the dolls in his bedroom in the burgomaster's mansion. See [[Undiscovered Places/The Town of Vallaki|The Town of Vallaki]], N7.
