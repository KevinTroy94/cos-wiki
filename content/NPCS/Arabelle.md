Young Vistani girl, daughter of [[Norella]], a jeweler at the camp near the [[River Ivlis]]. She went missing in the night after being told she couldn't go fishing with [[Krogarov]], and had been gone a full day by the time the party learned of it.

[[Laszlo, The Storyteller]] offered the party safe passage beyond [[The Mists]] in exchange for bringing her back. [[Alenka]] later admitted to having seen her recently in Barovia village, with Krogarov and a wagon carrying two heavy sacks. Her whereabouts beyond that remain unknown.
