A wounded treasure hunter the party encountered on the road to Barovia village, bearing two bite marks from the large [[Wolf]] creatures in the woods. He had been travelling with [[Ezmerelda d'Avenir]] and [[Oleksi Kurgari]] when their group was attacked. By his account, he and Oleksi fought while Oleksi tried to abandon him to the wolves. Ezmerelda killed one of them and then wasn't seen again.

The party patched him up. He thanked them and went on his way, mentioning a [[Hunters Tower]] he needed to return to.
