A scrawny boy in ill-fitting plate armour, picked up along with the rest of the party by [[Garlan Shepard]] on the road north of Neverwinter. The party came to know him as Red, for his wispy ginger hair and boyish hints of a moustache.

When the fog swallowed the road and the party climbed down to investigate, Red refused to leave the wagon. He managed, with some effort, to stay inside while everything fell apart around him. When [[Stanford]] broke free and the wagon lost its axle, the party hoisted Red onto [[Keneticut]] and brought him in tow down the road.

At the fork, Red split from the group and headed toward [[The Last Gasp]], while the others followed the Old Svalich Road.
