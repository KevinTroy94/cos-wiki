The baker of [[Vallaki]], who runs [[The Merry Crust]]. She petitioned [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]] in the town square because her flour cart had not come in a month, and was told "Great news! We can work off some of this winter fat and spare our purses for the time being! All will be well!"

When [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] visited her shop, she stood behind the counter floury to the elbows, with a smile that didn't quite reach her eyes. Her flour comes from Krezk, to the west, and nothing has arrived for weeks. There has been no word from the village, either.

When [[Retnuh]] came looking for answers, she told him of [[Sunset Lodge]] and the kinds of punishment meted out in Vallaki, and of Kozlov Manor, the empty manor of the first family sent out to the lodge, three years ago. Someone has agreed to buy it, and she assumed the party was part of that group.
