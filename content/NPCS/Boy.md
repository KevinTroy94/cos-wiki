[[Bildrath Cantomere]]'s boy. A large, heavyset lad who helped [[Krogarov]] repair his wagon and re-shoe his horse, and assisted him out with three large sacks.

> [!dmspoiler]
> His name is Parriwimple, Bildrath's nephew. Despite his imposing size he is gentle and simple-minded, devoted to his uncle and eager to please. He works as stock boy and general muscle for the mercantile. He is not malicious, just obedient, and unlikely to cause the party trouble unless Bildrath directs him to.
