A name in the register of burials at [[St. Andral's Church]], with a cause of death written beside it: _a fall._

A pocket watch engraved with the name **P. Hask** sits on the shelf of mementos at [[Sunset Lodge]]. When a [[Sunset Gargoyle]] seized [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] and carried him up into the sky, he realised that Pieter Hask had not fallen. He had been taken up.
