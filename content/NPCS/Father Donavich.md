The priest who keeps the church in Barovia village, a gray, sagging building on a rise against the roots of the pillar stone that holds up Castle Ravenloft. [[Ireena Kolyana]] asked that her father, [[Kolyan Indirovich]], be buried before she leaves the village, and the party came to the church hoping Donavich would bless the burial in the name of the Morninglord.

They found him kneeling before a claw-scarred altar in a ruined chapel, surrounded by dozens of candles burning against the dark. He had worn through the knees of his vestments. He droned the same prayer over and over, "Morninglord, Morninglord, shine your light upon your son and see us through this darkness", unclasping his hands only to tug the bell rope beside him. His forearms are covered in pocked, circular cuts. Up close, they are teeth marks.

"Forgive me," he told the party, his voice rough and hoarse. "I don't often get guests through those doors anymore. My name is Father Donavich. I keep this church, for whatever that's worth these days."

Then a voice rose up from beneath the floor: "Father! I'm starving!" Donavich answered it with prayer. Pressed by [[Ignacious Teegan]], he admitted it was his son, [[Doru]], who has been "unwell" and hasn't left the undercroft since he came back from the castle. Donavich has tried to feed him, but he is always hungry for more.

He is convinced the Morninglord sent the party to him, and begged them to save his boy.

> [!dmspoiler]
> Donavich (LG male human **acolyte**) has been praying throughout the night. His voice is hoarse and weak. He is, in a word, insane. A little more than a year ago, his twenty-year-old son [[Doru]] and several other villagers stormed Castle Ravenloft in revolt, having been lured there by a wizard in black robes who came to Barovia from a faraway land (see [[Wizard]]). By all accounts, the wizard died by Strahd's hand, and so too did Doru, who returned to his father as a vampire spawn. Donavich was able to trap his son in the church's undercroft, where he remains to this day.
>
> Donavich prays day and night, hoping that the gods will tell him how to save Doru without destroying him. If the characters seem intent on slaying Doru, Donavich does his best to stop them. If Doru dies, Donavich falls to the floor and weeps inconsolably, overcome with despair.
>
> Donavich knows the following useful information:
> - Ireena Kolyana isn't the natural daughter of Kolyan Indirovich. Although Ireena never knew, Kolyan found her at the edge of the Svalich Woods near the Pillarstone of Ravenloft. She was but a girl then and seemed to have no memory of her past. Kolyan adopted her and loved her dearly.
> - Every night at midnight, the spirits of dead adventurers rise up out of the church graveyard, forming a silent procession as they walk the road toward Castle Ravenloft.
>
> **Funeral for the Burgomaster.** Donavich presses the characters into helping him bury the burgomaster in the cemetery at dawn, offering prayers to the Morninglord in exchange for Kolyan Indirovich's deliverance from Barovia. Once Kolyan is put in the ground, Donavich suggests that Ireena be taken as far from Castle Ravenloft as possible: the Abbey of Saint Markovia in [[The Village of Krezk]] or, failing that, the fortified town of [[The Town of Vallaki]]. Donavich is unaware that the abbey, once a bastion of good, has become a den of evil.
