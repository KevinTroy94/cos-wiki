[[Vallaki]]'s coffin maker. [[Father Lucian]] recognised his name, **Van der Voort**, when [[Edric Storm|Edric]] and [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] asked about it.

At his [[Coffin Maker's Shop|shop]] he told the party to go away. When Ignacious kicked the door down, Henrik was knocked prone beneath him and Edric. He begged them not to go upstairs: "They are up there." He wouldn't say who had put him up to stealing the bones: "She will kill me if I talk to you." Even Edric's threat, "I am a cleric of death, and I will show you the meaning of fear," didn't move him: "Nothing you can do to me will make me fear you more than the dread woman."

When they took their eyes off him, he pushed past [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] and ran into the street, calling out to the townsfolk to get inside and lock their doors, and fled terrified toward the town's eastern exit. The bones of St. Andral were hidden in his shop.
