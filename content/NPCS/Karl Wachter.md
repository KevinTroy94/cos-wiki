The younger of the Wachter brothers, young nobles and brash drunks who spend their nights at the [[Blue Water Inn]] looking for trouble. Karl is the louder and more obnoxious of the two, broader and redder than his brother [[Nikolai Wachter|Nikolai]], his shirt half untucked and his grin loose with wine.

He leaned in and belched in [[Retnuh]]'s ear. Retnuh punched him between the legs, and [[Volenta Popofsky|Volenta]] broke a bottle of wine over his head and kicked him to the floor, unconscious. Mayhem broke out in the bar.
