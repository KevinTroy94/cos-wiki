Adopted daughter of the late Burgomaster [[Kolyan Indirovich]] and sister of [[Ismark The Lesser]].

When [[Virro]] and [[Edric Storm]] reached the Burgomaster's manor in Barovia village, they found it besieged by forces loyal to [[Strahd von Zarovich]]. Ireena met them at the door and questioned them, taking them for agents of the dark lord. Once they convinced her otherwise, she let them inside, where her father's body lay in state.

She is not unknown to Strahd. At [[The Blood of the Vine]], one of the Vistani women asked Ismark how many times his sister had been visited by a mysterious "he", and he stormed out. Her father's undelivered letter says she has been bitten by a vampyr these past nights. And on the road outside the village, Strahd named her to [[Retnuh]] as the one person he wanted brought to Castle Ravenloft, to be "looked after properly." Retnuh agreed to bring her once [[Arabelle]] is found.

Ireena asked the party to escort her out of Barovia village toward a safer town, but only once her father had been buried. She, Virro, and Edric set out for the church, hoping [[Father Donavich]] would bless the burial in the name of the Morninglord.

At the centre of town they fell in with [[Ignacious Teegan]] and [[Vesper Kasimov]], and then Retnuh rode up on the [[Nightmare]] Buccephalus. At the mention of Strahd's name, Ireena's expression closed off entirely. When Retnuh asked her to come with him to Castle Ravenloft, her answer came without hesitation:

> "I will go to that castle when I am ready to bring the fight to him myself. Not before."

A short while later, it was Ireena who turned the horse into something useful. She proposed that she and Virro use Buccephalus as a channel back to Strahd, feeding him whatever misinformation the party wanted him to believe.

When the church collapsed, [[Virro]] pulled Ireena from the rubble free of any major injuries. Her father's burial went ahead in the church grounds, in the coffin she and Ismark had made for him.

On the road between two halves of an old graveyard, she fought alongside the party against the [[Zombie|zombies]] that rose from the graves.

> [!dmspoiler]
> Kolyan found Ireena as a girl at the edge of the Svalich Woods near the Pillarstone of Ravenloft, with no memory of her past. He adopted her and loved her dearly. Ireena never knew. [[Father Donavich]] knows.
>
> Ireena carries the reincarnated soul of Tatyana, Strahd's lost love, which is why she looks exactly like her and why Strahd is obsessed with her.
