Adopted daughter of [[Arjun Senostrov]] and [[Oskar Senostrov]], who run the [[The Gallows Oak]] inn. Quiet and watchful, she spends much of her time working on a large embroidered tapestry that she plans to line one wall of the inn. It depicts a long procession of distinct travellers, stitched hand in hand, each with an X across their eyes.

When [[Virro]] sat with her, she agreed to add him to it. Once she finished his caricature, the tapestry slid from the table, the figures seeming to drag him down with them.

Her father was among the peasants incited to riot by the [[Wizard]] who challenged [[Strahd von Zarovich]] the previous year. He did not survive it.
