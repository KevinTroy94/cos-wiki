The chamberlain of Castle Ravenloft. Tall and dark-skinned, with long hair, dressed in fine black and deep purple, the fabric cut close and precise. He speaks with a voice of strange, foreign silk.

He appeared behind [[Father Donavich]] as the priest began the eulogy at [[Kolyan Indirovich]]'s burial. [[Ismark The Lesser|Ismark]] pointed him out to [[Edric Storm]] as Rahadin, the castle chamberlain. "You are doubtless tired, Father. Take a seat," he said, laying a hand on Donavich's shoulder as his eyes glinted purple, and Donavich sat. Edric recognised the spell for what it was: *suggestion*.

Rahadin then gave the eulogy himself, beneath "the glorious shadow of the Pillarstone of Ravenloft", paying mocking tribute to "a Barovian of little renown" while the mourners looked on in discomfort.

> [!dmspoiler]
> Rahadin, the dusk elf chamberlain of Castle Ravenloft, has served Strahd's family faithfully for nearly five hundred years. He is Strahd's eternal servant, a longtime comrade-in-arms, and a ruthless warrior who has killed thousands in his lifetime.
