The innkeeper of the [[Blue Water Inn]] in [[Vallaki]], which he runs with his wife, [[Danika Martikov]], and their family.

He poured for [[Retnuh]] and [[Volenta Popofsky|Volenta]], and when Retnuh asked after the wine, he sighed: no shipment had come from the Wizard of Wines, the winery out west, in far too long. He would gladly hire adventurers to go and see what's wrong. His relatives run the place, and if the party can find out what's going on, they'll have free room and board at the inn for as long as they like.
