Brother of [[Arjun Senostrov]] and co-proprietor of the [[The Gallows Oak]] inn, along with their adopted daughter [[Adeline]]. He and Arjun agreed to put the party up for the night in exchange for some labour and pointed them toward the Vistani camp near the [[River Ivlis]].

He and his brother seem to have no particular quarrel with [[Strahd von Zarovich]]. They are wary of what lurks in the mists but not panicked by it.
