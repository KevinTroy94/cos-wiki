The elder of the Wachter brothers, young nobles and brash drunks who spend their nights at the [[Blue Water Inn]] looking for trouble. Nikolai is lean and pale, with the glassy, amused look of a man who finds everything slightly funny. He was there when his brother [[Karl Wachter|Karl]] was knocked out by [[Retnuh]] and [[Volenta Popofsky|Volenta]].

In the brawl that followed, he tried to kill [[Retnuh]] with a barstool.

Their mother, Fiona Wachter, is an influential figure in town, but her sons never talk about her."
