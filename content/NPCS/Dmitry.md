A knight of the [[Order of Contentment]], the "smiling knight". He wore a dark surcoat with a grinning gold sun across the chest, a green gorget at the collar, and a bascinet whose visor bore a wide, painted smile. He fought like a brawler: heavy, close, all shoulders and pommel, swinging his longsword as though trying to knock a door off its hinges.

The party found him on the road to Vallaki, duelling [[Ernest Festibule]], and he called on them for help: "Citizens! Help me apprehend this man! He is a fugitive and a vagabond!" By his own account he was the law, the Baron's man. By Festibule's account, the two had had many bouts.

[[Retnuh]] chose the other side, charging Dmitry and knocking him to the ground, and Festibule beheaded him. The smiling bascinet rolled to a stop on the gravel, its painted grin still turned to the sky. The party burned his body. [[Virro]] took his sword, but Retnuh made him swear to return it to that spot when he was done with it.
