A kindly priest of the Morninglord who tends [[St. Andral's Church]] in [[Vallaki]]. He asked [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] and [[Edric Storm|Edric]] about their faith and, sensing they were men of faith, asked for their help: the bones of St. Andral are missing from the church's crypt. Without them, Vallaki's protection against the undead won't function, and there could be vampires among the townsfolk at this very moment.
