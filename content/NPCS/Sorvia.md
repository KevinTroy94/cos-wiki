One of three Vistani women at the [[The Blood of the Vine]] tavern in Barovia village, alongside [[Mirabelle]] and [[Alenka]]. Tall and broad-shouldered, with dirty blonde hair pulled back loosely and cut short. Her voice is unusually deep for a woman, low and steady, and she carries herself like a hunter, practical, watchful, unbothered by much.

As the night wore on, she and [[Retnuh]] found themselves talking more to each other than to anyone else, something easy and mutual sparking between them. She offered to show him her room upstairs and the two retired together.

Afterward, she let slip that [[Alenka]] had seen [[Arabelle]] and [[Krogarov]] recently in town, with a wagon and two heavy sacks. Their conversation was cut short when spectral blue light appeared in the street below and Retnuh rushed out to investigate.

It later emerged that Sorvia and her companions own the [[The Blood of the Vine]].
