A Vistani jeweler at the camp near the [[River Ivlis]] and mother of [[Arabelle]]. When [[Ignacious Teegan]] came to her wagon looking for information, she let him in reluctantly, watching him with the wary, unfocused eyes of someone who had been drinking for a while.

"I can't pay you," she said, before he'd even asked. She said it again a moment later, as if the words had become a reflex. Then she sank into a heap of cushions and reached for an open bottle of wine on the floor, drinking straight from it without looking up.

Pressed gently, she admitted Arabelle had been missing for a full day, likely vanishing in the night after being told she couldn't go fishing with [[Krogarov]].
