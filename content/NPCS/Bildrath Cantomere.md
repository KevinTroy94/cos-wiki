Proprietor of [[Bildrath's Mercantile]] in Barovia village. Standoffish and unhelpful by default, directing [[Ignacious Teegan]] to a list of goods he could buy rather than answering any questions.

Eventually he relented and shared that [[Krogarov]] had passed through with a wagon needing repair and a horse to be re-shoed. His [[Boy]] had helped fix the wagon and shoe the horse, and Krogarov had left carrying three large sacks.
