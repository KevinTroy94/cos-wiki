The son of [[Father Donavich]], kept in the undercroft beneath the church in Barovia village. The party first heard him as a desperate voice rising up through the floorboards: "Father! I'm starving!" 

According to his father, Doru is "unwell" and hasn't left the undercroft since he came back from the castle. Donavich has tried to feed him, but he is always hungry for more. His father's forearms are covered in teeth marks.

The party learned that Doru has been stricken with the curse of vampirism. [[Ignacious Teegan]] recalled a story of a vampire who became a monk by dedicating himself to the light of Lathander, and told them that a strict regimen, or death, is the only cure.

Donavich begged the party to save his boy, and they went down into the [[Barovia Village Church|undercroft]] to confront him. A gaunt shape hunched in the far corner shifted the moment they stepped off the last stair, chains gliding across the floor as he came to them. "I can smell your blood," he rasped. "Have you come to feed me?"

[[Ignacious Teegan]] offered him a way out: swear to harm no person again, hunt animals or take only from those who offer willingly, and the party would help him. Doru's eyes never left him as he spoke, fixed not on his face but on his throat, tracking the pulse beneath the skin. Then he lunged.

[[Retnuh]] charged him first and was bitten before [[Edric Storm]] and [[Virro]] threw Doru off him. The party used the pillars and the length of Doru's chain to hold him back, luring him back and forth with many close calls. Eventually he found a little slack, leapt to the ceiling, and dug his claws into Virro's shoulder. Before he could bite, Retnuh's axe found purchase in his side, and at that moment the groaning timbers gave way and the floor collapsed on them all.
