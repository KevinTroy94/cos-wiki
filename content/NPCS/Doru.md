The son of [[Father Donavich]], kept in the undercroft beneath the church in Barovia village. The party first heard him as a desperate voice rising up through the floorboards: "Father! I'm starving!" Chains dragged quickly across the floor below, and something underneath watched them through the cracks.

According to his father, Doru is "unwell" and hasn't left the undercroft since he came back from the castle. Donavich has tried to feed him, but he is always hungry for more. His father's forearms are covered in teeth marks.

The party learned that Doru has been stricken with the curse of vampirism. [[Ignacious Teegan]] recalled a story of a vampire who became a monk by dedicating himself to the light of Lathander, and told them that a strict regimen, or death, is the only cure.

Donavich begged the party to save his boy. They went down into the undercroft to confront him.

> A little more than a year ago, Doru and several other villagers stormed Castle Ravenloft in revolt, having been lured there by a wizard in black robes (see [[Wizard]]). By all accounts, the wizard died by Strahd's hand, and so too did Doru, who returned to his father as a vampire spawn. Donavich was able to trap his son in the church's undercroft, where he remains to this day.
>
> Doru is a **vampire spawn** sent by Strahd to torment Donavich and cast down the church. He cries out to his father at all hours. Doru is starved for blood and brave enough to attack a lone character. If the characters approach as a group, he does his best to avoid them while hissing, "I can smell your blood!" If they cut off his escape, he lunges forth and attacks.
>
> If the characters restrain Doru and either promise him blood or threaten to destroy him, or if they kill him and then raise him from the dead, he recounts the events that led to his downfall.
