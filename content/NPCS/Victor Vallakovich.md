The son of [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]]. A boy of perhaps seventeen, so thin that his coat hangs off him like a coat on a peg, sickly and pale, the colour of old candle wax.

[[Retnuh]] found him on the front step of the burgomaster's mansion during the attack on Vallaki, kneeling to coax one of his father's [[Hell Hound|hounds]] inside with a scrap of meat. When Retnuh came through the gates with a saber-toothed tiger on his heels, Victor snatched a battered notebook from his coat, flipped through it with shaking hands, and unleashed a wave of thunder from his palm that flung stones across the courtyard and knocked Retnuh unconscious. "Oh no."
