A voice rang out from behind Edric "Hand over the bones, little man"
The voice came from directly behind Edric, low and warm, like velvet drawn over a blade. It carried with it an unhurried amusement.
"or everyone dies"
He turned. A woman stood at the foot of the stairs in the dim light of Henrik’s shop, and Edric understood at once that she had been standing there for some time. She was tall and slender, her skin the grey-blue of old moonlight, and her ears came to long, elegant points hung with heavy golden earrings. Her eyes were amber, lined in black, and fixed on him with open, patient interest. Black hair fell to her hips in thick, glossy waves, and across her brow sat a headdress of red silk set with a single jewel. A collar of dark stones lay against her throat. Her gown was the red of fresh blood, long-sleeved and trailing behind her across the floorboards in a heavy train, and the nails on her pale fingers were black and curved like claws.

The woman was Anastrasya Karelova.

"I wont be doing that" Defied Edric. You will leave this place at once. 

The woman in red smirked and drew a large sword from behind her dress, its tip landing on the floor with a loud thunk, it was impossibly big to be concealed behind her slender frame. 

"This is your last chance for peace" "Your last chance to negotiate."

Edric was resolute 
“Vile creatures, leave this house at once!”

Radiance burst from him in a wave, white and gold, flooding the room and climbing the stairs. A deep, rolling boom shook the whole building, and dust rained from the rafters.

Meanwhile Ignacious whipped around to see a man had risen from one of the coffins. He was bedraggled and rumpled, a vampire with mussed black hair, the shaved side of his head marked with dark tattoos and a trim moustache and goatee framing a lined face. His fangs showed long and white as he stretched in front of the window and let out a vast, creaking yawn.

He bore long fangs as he stretched in front of the window and yawned out "Is it time already?"

He looked expectantly at Edric who tried to convince him to get back into his coffin.

It was almost working until the boom of Edrics spell rattled the room and alerted the curious Vampire Spawn to Ignacious' intentions. Springing into action, Ignacious lifted one of the coffin lids and plunged his stake into the heart of a still resting vampire within, causing it to burst into a cloud of ash and dust. 

Edrics spell had worked on at least one of the vampires remaining in the coffins, who scrambled out and headed for the window, crashing through the glass and out onto the street in an attempt to avoid the holy presence. 

In the confusion, Edric seized an opportunity to tear open another coffin and plunge his stake into the heart of another vampire, who woke up in time to grab the stake, before Ignacious hammered the stake with his other hand, driving it into her heart regardless. 

Downstairs Anastrasya mocked Edric and pretended his spell affected her before laughing at him,  Edric  seized her momentary distraction and made his way for the lower window, as he did, Anastrasya vanished into a small cloud of mist and flowed like a cursed wind between him and the window. Edric dodged under her and made his way for the front door, where he handed the bones to Ireena, who was waiting to receive them. Ireena began sprinting toward the church and Anastrasya headed after her. Anastrasya regarded Ireena with an intense look of familiarity and hatred as she began hurling magick at her heels. 

The vampire who jumped out the window ran over to a house across the street, rapped on the door of the townhouse and begged for help. Once the door opened and the owner said "Come inside where its safe", he plunged his claws into the mans neck and fled inside.
