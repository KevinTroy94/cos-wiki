A voice rang out from directly behind Edric. “Hand over the bones, little man.” It was low and warm, like velvet drawn over a blade, and it carried with it an unhurried amusement. “Or everyone dies.”

He turned. A woman stood at the foot of the stairs in the dim light of [[Coffin Maker's Shop|Henrik’s shop]], and Edric understood at once that she had been standing there for some time. She was tall and slender, her skin the grey-blue of old moonlight, and her ears came to long, elegant points hung with heavy golden earrings. Her eyes were amber, lined in black, and fixed on him with open, patient interest. Black hair fell to her hips in thick, glossy waves, and across her brow sat a headdress of red silk set with a single jewel. A collar of dark stones lay against her throat. Her gown was the red of fresh blood, long-sleeved and trailing behind her across the floorboards in a heavy train, and the nails on her pale fingers were black and curved like claws.

The woman was [[Anastrasya Karelova]].

![[image-removebg-preview(14).png]]

“I won’t be doing that,” Edric defied her. “You will leave this place at once.”

The woman in red smirked and drew a large sword from behind her dress, its tip landing on the floor with a loud thunk. It was impossibly big to have been concealed behind her slender frame.

“This is your last chance for peace. Your last chance to negotiate.”

Edric was resolute. “Vile creatures, leave this house at once!”

Radiance burst from him in a wave, white and gold, flooding the room and climbing the stairs. A deep, rolling boom shook the whole building, and dust rained from the rafters.

Meanwhile, Ignacious whipped around to see that a man had risen from one of the coffins. He was bedraggled and rumpled, a vampire with mussed black hair, the shaved side of his head marked with dark tattoos, and a trim moustache and goatee framing a lined face. His fangs showed long and white as he stretched in front of the window and let out a vast, creaking yawn.

![[image-removebg-preview(15).png]]

“Is it time already?”

He looked expectantly at Edric, who tried to convince him to get back into his coffin.

It was almost working until the boom of Edric’s spell rattled the room and alerted the curious [[Vampire Spawn|vampire spawn]] to Ignacious’ intentions. Springing into action, Ignacious lifted one of the coffin lids and plunged his stake into the heart of a still-resting vampire within, causing it to burst into a cloud of ash and dust.

Edric’s spell had worked on at least one of the vampires remaining in the coffins, who scrambled out and headed for the window, crashing through the glass and out onto the street in an attempt to escape the holy presence.

In the confusion, Edric seized the opportunity to tear open another coffin and plunge his stake into the heart of another vampire, who woke in time to grab the stake before Ignacious hammered it with his other hand, driving it into her heart regardless.

Downstairs, Anastrasya mocked Edric, pretending his spell had affected her before laughing at him. Edric seized her momentary distraction and made for the lower window. As he did, Anastrasya vanished into a small cloud of mist and flowed like a cursed wind between him and the window. Edric dodged under her and made for the front door, where he handed the bones to [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]], who was waiting to receive them. Ireena began sprinting toward the church, and Anastrasya went after her. She regarded Ireena with an intense look of familiarity and hatred as she began hurling magic at her heels.

The vampire who had jumped out of the window ran over to a townhouse across the street, rapped on the door and begged for help. Once the door opened and the owner said, “Come inside where it’s safe,” he plunged his claws into the man’s neck and fled inside.

At once, the sound of bells could be heard all around the town as the [[Order of Contentment]] sounded the alarm.

![[image-removebg-preview(6).png]]

Ignacious was suddenly surrounded by three vampire spawn. As they fell on him, he found his determination and his resolve, calling out, “No better stand... for the son of Ilmater!” Light burst from him as he crashed into them like a wave.

Across the town, Virro and Retnuh looked at the red plate armour and the bodies they had exhumed. Covered in dirt and with shovels in hand, their guilt was beyond doubt. They began trying to persuade [[Father Lucian]], reasoning with him that the investigation was necessary, even as the Order of Contentment closed in.

![[image-removebg-preview(16).png]]

Four knights of the Order of Contentment found them in the church courtyard and asked them to explain. One knight, whose bascinet bore a long amber plume, led the questioning. Retnuh attempted to plead with them.

“Don’t you have people in this town you care about? Friends? Mothers? Brothers? Girlfriends? Boyfriends? Polycules? Dogs? Fish? Do you keep fish? What’s a fish you keep? Trout? Can you keep trout? Do you guys have trout here? How big is a pet trout? Nah, you probably keep goldfish...”

Long after Retnuh’s original point was lost to time, the Order of Contentment stood in a moment of stunned silence, before the man with the plume barked, “Seize them.” Virro used his shovel to throw grave dirt in his face, shouted “Eat shit,” and bounded into the distance. Retnuh followed immediately after, quickly gaining pace and outstriding him.

The pair bounded through the streets with the Order of Contentment hot on their heels. Virro made his way through a small street market, while Retnuh veered off and used a market stall to climb up through a second-storey window. He ran straight through to the back of the house, past some bewildered Barovians, and out of the back window. Retnuh landed on a roof and crossed the rooftops until he fell through a thin, rotted one.

![[Weathered Carnival Wagon Sign.png]]

The creature that met him there was a chained saber-toothed tiger. It chased him out of the stockyard and down the street until he reached the burgomaster’s mansion.

![[test1.png]]

At the mansion, Retnuh came skidding through the gates with a saber-toothed tiger on his heels and found someone standing in his way. On the front step was a boy of perhaps seventeen, so thin that his coat hung off him like a coat on a peg. [[Victor Vallakovich]] was sickly and pale, the colour of old candle wax. He was kneeling in front of one of his father’s [[Hell Hound|hounds]] with a scrap of meat held out in his palm, coaxing the huge animal toward the door, as it seemed distressed by the bells and the sound of panic in the town.

He barely had time to look up. “Who goes there?” he managed, in a thin, startled voice, before the tiger was upon them. The hound slid between the boy and the tiger, while Victor scrambled backward, snatched a battered notebook from inside his coat and began to flip through it with shaking hands.

A great wave of thunder burst outward from his palm with a deafening _crack_, and the force of it slammed through the courtyard, flinging stones in every direction. Retnuh bore the brunt of it and was lifted off his feet.

“Oh no,” managed a surprised Victor, as Retnuh fell unconscious.

![[image-removebg-previewvictor.png]]

Virro rounded a corner in the burning dark and nearly ran into a knot of guards in sun-faced helms. Between two of them, one gripping each arm, a short figure in a shoulderless gold dress dangled, her large brown boots barely touching the cobbles. The horned skull mask turned toward him as he skidded to a halt, and even through the bone, he could hear her grinning.

“Oh, _hello_,” said [[Volenta Popofsky|Volenta]].

He drove his boot into the nearest guard’s knee and launched himself off it. The man folded with a grunt and let go of her arm. Volenta, who had been waiting for exactly this, whooped with delight. Her freed hand shot out and drove a fist into the second guard’s helm with a ringing _clang_. They pounded down the street together. The chaos lit their backs, and the shouts of the Order rose and fell behind them. Then three more guards stepped out of an alley directly ahead, longswords drawn and visors grinning.

“Want to be useful?”

He pointed.

Volenta clapped her hands once in pure joy. “Oh, I _do_,” she said, and went.

She moved in a way that didn’t match her size, quick and low and utterly unbothered, slipping inside the first sword’s reach before it had finished swinging. The street filled with the clash of steel and the sound of someone enjoying herself enormously. Virro kept going.

Meanwhile, Ignacious faced the vampire who had risen yawning from his coffin. In the melee, which had spilled out into the smoking street, it caught Ignacious by the throat and lifted him, and its cold fingers closed like a vice. The roar of the burning town thinned to a dull hum. The edges of the world went grey. His sword fell with his arm and found the crook of the vampire’s elbow, and in a single stroke the arm came away clean. Black blood sprayed the cobbles, the grip at his throat fell open, and Ignacious dropped to his knees, dragging in a breath that tasted of ash.

Out of the smoke and din came [[Keneticut]].

Ignacious grabbed the saddle and swung himself up onto her back. The creature wasn’t finished. Its remaining hand shot out, clawing at his cloak, and it hauled with all its monstrous strength, dragging him half out of the saddle. Keneticut’s hind legs came up and snapped back, breaking the vampire’s neck.

As Ignacious rode off, he saw the vampire’s neck right itself.

Anastrasya swept through the burning streets in her red gown, hurling bolts of lightning at Ireena as she ran with the bones of Saint Andral clutched to her chest. Each bolt split the dark with a white-blue crack and scorched the cobbles where Ireena had been a heartbeat before. Edric ran after them both, his holy symbol swinging at his neck. On the way, he passed a man locked in a pillory in the middle of the road, his head and wrists trapped in the wood, a smiling sun mask strapped over his face. Edric skidded to a halt and began tearing at the lock.

He had barely begun when a knight in a sun-faced helm fell upon him, and Edric had to drop everything and run.

“Don’t leave me! They’ll kill me, the vampires will kill me!”

Edric abandoned him. It was the hardest thing he had done that night.

One by one, the party made their way toward the church, and somewhere along the way they lost track of Anastrasya. Ireena arrived first, breathless, and pushed the bones into Father Lucian’s hands. The Order of Contentment knight who had been following them followed suit, and passed through the doors of [[St. Andral's Church|St. Andral’s]] with the rest.

Before long, Anastrasya was at the door behind them.

She stopped on the threshold, and it held her. She could not cross uninvited. Then she smiled, turned, and fixed her amber eyes on a passer-by in the street, a frightened townsman who had been hurrying home. The man stopped. His face went slack. Obediently, he came to the church doors, opened them wide, and spoke the words.

“Please. Come in,” he mumbled, before falling to the ground.

Anastrasya stepped across the threshold.

Ignacious, Edric and Virro formed a line to hold her off, and the Order’s knight stood with them, shoulder to shoulder, an unlikely ally. Behind them, Ireena and Father Lucian worked through the ritual.

Meanwhile, Retnuh came to amid a scene of gore. The tiger had apparently been killed, and the hound was lying still on its side. Retnuh wasted no time and ran into the street. There he encountered the one-armed vampire who had faced Ignacious. They battled fiercely, and Retnuh fell unconscious again.

Retnuh awoke once more to see Volenta looking down at him. The vampires that had been there before were nowhere to be seen.

From the direction of the church, a white flash of light erupted and illuminated Volenta’s smile.
