[[Retnuh]] followed the ghostly procession through the streets of Barovia, rain beginning to needle down through the fog, cold and thin, hissing faintly where it struck the cobblestones. The spirits paid the weather no mind at all. They drifted on in that same loose, wandering formation, all of them moving with the same slow, deliberate purpose. Retnuh kept pace beside them, soaked through within minutes, watching his own breath fog in the cold while theirs did not.

The procession carried him clear out of the village and onto the open road beyond, rain falling steadier now, until the fog ahead parted around the shape of a wagon standing motionless at the roadside. It was black, lacquered so dark it seemed to drink the little light there was, its wheels and fittings wrought in iron and silver scrollwork, a crest worked into its door that Retnuh didn't recognize. No driver sat at the front. No one waited inside. It simply stood there in the rain, empty and still.

Harnessed to it was a horse unlike any Retnuh had seen. Ash grey, its coat seeming to smolder faintly at the edges, its eyes burning a deep, steady orange in the gloom. It stood perfectly calm as the ghostly host passed directly through the wagon itself, continuing on down the road toward the distant silhouette of Castle Ravenloft rising through the mist on the hillside beyond.

Retnuh stared, something in him coming loose at the sight of it, and found himself grinning despite the cold and the rain and the impossibility of it all. He crossed to the horse without much thought, digging a handful of nuts from his pack and offering them up on an open palm. On closer inspection the horses mane was amber-bright and hot to the touch.
![[Pasted image 20260924100312.png|229]]

The horse dipped its head and ate from his hand without hesitation, and Retnuh felt a faint warmth pass across his skin as it did, the nuts seeming to roast lightly right there in his palm before the horse took them.

"Wonderful beast, isn't she."

The voice came from behind him, smooth and unhurried, carrying an old world cadence that made every word sound deliberate, almost performed. Retnuh didn't turn. He stayed with the horse, running a hand along its neck, and answered the stranger without looking back.

They spoke for a long while like that. The man behind him was tall, Retnuh gathered from the shape of the shadow thrown long across the wet road, dressed in fine dark clothes that belonged to another century entirely, his voice carrying the particular stillness of someone who never seemed to be in any hurry at all. Retnuh only caught glimpses from the corner of his eye. Pale skin. Dark hair swept back. Eyes that seemed to hold their own small light in them, the same way the horse's did.
![[Pasted image 20260924100414.png]]
The stranger offered to help him up onto the horse's back, and Retnuh let him.

As they talked and Retnuh adjusted to the saddle, the man spoke of the curse laid over this land, old and patient and without mercy, and of the mists that wringed it, saying they had a way of unraveling a person's memory the longer they lingered in Barovia. Retnuh found, turning the thought over, that he was already struggling to place much of his life from before the mists had taken him.

"Come to Castle Ravenloft," the man said. "Dine with me, and we will see what of your memory can be recovered."

Retnuh, loosened by the strangeness of the night and the warmth still lingering in his palm from the strange horse's breath, told him of Arabelle, and of Krogarov, and of the party's search for both of them through the rain soaked, joyless town.

"I will have my people watch for them both," the stranger said, and there was something almost pleased in how readily he offered it. "And the horse. Buccephalus may carry you for as long as she chooses to stay at your side."

Retnuh asked him then about the curse itself, and whether there was anyone in this land looking out for him at all.

The stranger was quiet for a moment before he answered. "There is a girl," he said. 
"[[Ireena Kolyana]]. Bring her to me at Castle Ravenloft, and I will see that she is looked after properly."

Retnuh agreed without much thought, telling him that after they had found [[Arabelle]] he would see her brought to the castle himself.

"Wonderful" he said calmly, and something in his voice settled, satisfied, as the rain went on falling around them both.

[[Strahd von Zarovich]] vanished into the night as Retnuh rode off back towards [[The Village of Barovia]] on the back of the [[Nightmare]]. The wagon quietly vanished into the mists behind him.
