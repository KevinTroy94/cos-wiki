The last thing any of them saw was the ceiling giving way, timber and stone raining down in a single collapsing breath, and then nothing.

[[Virro]] woke somewhere else entirely.
There was no floor beneath him, only water, black and still and endless in every direction, stretching to a horizon where the whole sky pressed down and sealed at the edges in the colour of amber, appearing to take the consistency of glass.![[Pasted image 20260930151142.png]]


A figure stood on the water some distance off. Draped in robes of grey and gold, with glowing eyes of amber. It regarded Virro with a warm voice that seemed to ring in his head.

"You are drowing. Trapped." rang the voice sonorously. Virros thoughts seem to turn to being trapped in Barovia. The figure seemed to smile formlessly. 

"Not here. And not within the mists. Within the stone and blessed rubble."
"I can give you strength," the figure went on. "Enough to pull stone from stone. Enough to save who can still be saved." A pause, and something in the stillness of it felt deliberate, weighed. "It will cost you nothing today but nothing is without a price."

"I sense within you a spark of divinity, lets nurture it together"

Virro mulled it for a moment and boldly took the outstretched hand of the robed figure. 
![[Pasted image 20260930151410.png]]


Virro awoke beneath the rubble and began to pull himself free. He felt the strength and vigour of youth surge through him as he easily and tirelessly lifted his companions from the earth. 

First he found [[Vesper Kasimov|Vesper]], though he didnt realise it at first, as what he found was the limp body of a raven the size of a man. He had a long beak, large inky black wings and wore vespers clothes. His head awash with gore from the bad fall he had taken. [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] was next to be pulled from the rubble, free of any major injuries and easy to wake. After a few moments of digging, [[Retnuh]], [[Edric Storm|Edric]] and [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] were found. Last to be found was [[Father Donavich|father donavich]], who was unconscious and on deaths door. The gang set about resuscitating him and he awoke.

Before he even commented on the collapse of the [[Barovia Village Church|church]] he was concerned about [[Doru]] and asked where his son had gone. Virro picked up a trail where someone had moved rocks before he woke, he was unable to find doru among the wreckage and realised he was picking up on Doru's trail. He told ignacious and Retnuh who headed off on their horses in a hurry.

Resolute in the wake of his ruined church, Donavich dusted himself off and climbed up to the only intact section of the church where he said "we will continue with the burial rites for [[Kolyan Indirovich|Kolyan]]. while your friends find my son."

Retnuh and Ignacious pass the [[Vistani|vistani]] girls and [[Ismark The Lesser|Ismarck]], who are carrying the body of Kolyan indirovich in the coffin that Ireena and Ismarck made. Retnuh reaches out his hand toward [[Sorvia]] for a fist-bump, who accepts with a wry smile
