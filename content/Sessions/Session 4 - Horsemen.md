The last thing any of them saw was the ceiling giving way, timber and stone raining down in a single collapsing breath, and then nothing.

[[Virro]] woke somewhere else entirely.

There was no floor beneath him, only water, black and still and endless in every direction, stretching to a horizon where the whole sky pressed down and sealed at the edges in the colour of amber, appearing to take the consistency of glass.

![[Pasted image 20260930151142.png]]

A figure stood on the water some distance off, draped in robes of grey and gold, with glowing eyes of amber. It regarded Virro with a warm voice that seemed to ring in his head.

"You are drowning. Trapped," rang the voice sonorously. Virro's thoughts seemed to turn to being trapped in Barovia. The figure seemed to smile formlessly.

"Not here. And not within the mists. Within the stone and blessed rubble."

"I can give you strength," the figure went on. "Enough to pull stone from stone. Enough to save who can still be saved." A pause, and something in the stillness of it felt deliberate, weighed. "It will cost you nothing today, but nothing is without a price."

"I sense within you a spark of divinity. Let's nurture it together."

Virro mulled it for a moment and boldly took the outstretched hand of the robed figure.

![[Pasted image 20260930151410.png]]

Virro awoke beneath the rubble and began to pull himself free. He felt the strength and vigour of youth surge through him as he easily and tirelessly lifted his companions from the earth.

First he found [[Vesper Kasimov|Vesper]], though he didn't realise it at first, as what he found was the limp body of a raven the size of a man. He had a long beak and large, inky black wings, and wore Vesper's clothes, his head awash with gore from the bad fall he had taken. [[Ireena Kolyana|Ireena]] was next to be pulled from the rubble, free of any major injuries and easy to wake. After a few moments of digging, [[Retnuh]], [[Edric Storm|Edric]] and [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] were found. Last to be found was [[Father Donavich]], unconscious and at death's door. The gang set about resuscitating him, and he awoke.

Before he even commented on the collapse of the [[Barovia Village Church|church]], he was concerned about [[Doru]] and asked where his son had gone. Virro was unable to find Doru among the wreckage, but he picked up a trail where someone had moved rocks before he woke, and realised it was Doru's. He told Ignacious and Retnuh, who headed off on their horses in a hurry.

Resolute in the wake of his ruined church, Donavich dusted himself off and climbed up to the only intact section of the church, where he said, "We will continue with the burial rites for [[Kolyan Indirovich|Kolyan]] while your friends find my son."

Retnuh and Ignacious passed the [[Vistani]] girls and [[Ismark The Lesser|Ismark]], who were carrying the body of Kolyan Indirovich in the coffin that Ireena and Ismark had made. Retnuh reached out his hand toward [[Sorvia]] for a fist-bump, which she accepted with a wry smile.

Several dour-looking, sallow-skinned [[Barovian|Barovians]] gathered for a hurried ceremony to bury Kolyan Indirovich. Father Donavich began the ceremony standing at the head of the grave.

"We gather here in the light of the Morninglord to honour a mighty and compassionate fallen friend to all."

Before he could continue the eulogy, a figure appeared behind him. He was tall and dark-skinned, with long hair, dressed in fine black and deep purple, the fabric cut close and precise in a style that belonged. Ismark pointed out to Edric that he was [[NPCS/Rahadin|Rahadin]], the castle chamberlain.

![[Pasted image 20260930234405.png]]

"You are doubtless tired, Father. Take a seat," he said, with a voice of strange, foreign silk.

His hand found Father Donavich's shoulder, his eyes glinted purple, and Father Donavich took a seat. Edric looked concerned. He was aware that this person had just cast *suggestion*.

"We are gathered here beneath the glorious shadow of the Pillarstone of Ravenloft to pay humble tribute to a Barovian of little renown," began the eulogy. A look of discomfort washed across the crowd, while the stranger seemed to revel in his mockery for what felt like an eternity.

Meanwhile, after a short time, Ignacious and Retnuh tracked Doru to just outside of town. He lay half in the ditch at the roadside, one leg bent wrong beneath him, breathing in short, wet gasps that rattled with every rise of his chest, the wound from Retnuh's axe deep and plainly visible. He managed to get to his knees before the pair. Retnuh chopped a branch from a dead tree and began paring it into a stake while they talked.

"This darkness, you can't control it. You almost killed us," mourned Ignacious.

"This will be easier. There will be peace. Stay still, lad," he said, attempting to hold the vampire down. Doru strained in Ignacious's grasp as he was pinned to the muddy ground. Retnuh drove the stake into his heart. Doru didn't scream. He only let out a long breath, something between relief and shock. Soon after, his features melted away and he dissolved into white ash in their hands, his last breath fading into the air with an unsettling rattle.

Back at the funeral, Rahadin finishes by offering a large sack of gold to Father Donavich for rebuilding the church. He says it will come with "some instruction on how best to redesign it" and that it should help to guide people on how to live their lives. Rahadin had the body of Vesper the wereraven brought to the side of kolyans coffin and carelessly kicked it into the grave to 'save on space'.

Rahadin attempted to speak with Edric and Virro who both told them their names were Retnuh. Rahadin, unamused left them in the graveyard as he walked off. As Rahadin left, virro mocked his dress sense, a comment that had Rahadin stop in his tracks before continuing on.  Each Barovian present gathered a handful of dirt and threw it into the grave. Once the numbers had dwindled and Rahadin had left, Ismarck began to give a eulogy of his own.
