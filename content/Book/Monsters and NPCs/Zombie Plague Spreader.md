

Necromancers who wish to sow as much toxic mayhem as possible often create zombie plague spreaders to do their dirty work for them.

> **Zombie Plague Spreader**
>
> *Medium Undead, Neutral Evil*
>
> ---
>
> **AC** 10 · **Initiative** +0 (10)
>
> **HP** 78 (12d8 + 24)
>
> **Speed** 30 ft.
>

> **Resistances** Necrotic
>
> **Immunities** Poison; Charmed, Exhaustion, Poisoned
>
> **Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 7
>
> **Languages** Understands Common plus one other language but can't speak
>
> **CR** 4 (XP 1,100; PB +2)
>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Undead Fortitude.*** If damage reduces the zombie to 0 Hit Points, it makes a Constitution saving throw (DC 5 plus the damage taken) unless the damage is Radiant or from a Critical Hit. On a successful save, the zombie drops to 1 Hit Point instead.
>
> ***Viral Aura.*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, any creature that starts its turn in a 10-foot Emanation originating from the zombie. *Failure:* The target has the Poisoned condition until the start of its next turn. While Poisoned, the target can't regain Hit Points. *Success:* The creature is immune to this zombie's Viral Aura for 24 hours.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The zombie makes two Slam attacks.
>
> ***Slam.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +5, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) Necrotic damage.
>
> ***Virulent Miasma (1/Day).*** *Constitution Saving Throw:* DC 12, each creature in a 30-foot Emanation originating from the zombie. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Poison damage. *Success:* Half damage. *Failure or Success:* If this damage reduces the target to 0 Hit Points, it dies and rises 1 minute later as a **Zombie**.
