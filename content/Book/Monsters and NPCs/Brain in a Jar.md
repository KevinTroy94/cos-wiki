

A brain in a jar is the gray matter of one who cheated death through magic or strange surgeries. It now forces its will on those around it.

>

>
> #### Traits
>
> ***Detect Intelligence.*** The brain magically senses the location of any creature within 300 feet of itself that has an Intelligence score of 3 or higher, regardless of interposing barriers.
>
> ***Magic Resistance.*** The brain has Advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> #### Actions
>
> ***Multiattack.*** The brain makes two Disrupting Burst attacks.
>
> ***Disrupting Burst.*** *Melee or Ranged Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. or range 120 ft. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) Psychic damage. The target can't take Reactions until the start of the brain's next turn, and it has Disadvantage on saving throws to maintain Concentration until the end of its next turn. *(Partly obscured by glare in the photo; check the reaction duration against the book.)*
>
> ***Mind Blast (Recharge 5–6).*** *Intelligence Saving Throw:* DC 14, each creature in a 60-foot Cone. *Failure:* 14 (4d6) Psychic damage, and the target has the Incapacitated condition until the end of the brain's next turn. *Success:* Half damage only.
>
> ***Spellcasting.*** The brain casts one of the following spells, requiring no spell components and using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (spell save DC 14):
>
> **At Will:** *Charm Person, Detect Thoughts, Mage Hand*
>
> **1/Day Each:** *Compulsion, Hold Person* (level 3 version)
