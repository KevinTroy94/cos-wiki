An organisation the party first heard of from [[Vesper Kasimov]], who was expected to deliver a package to them. Little more about them is known at the moment. They appear to operate in secret and oppose Strahd.

Known Members
-----------------------------
[[Vesper Kasimov]]


> [!dmspoiler]
> A secret society of wereravens that opposes Strahd, using ordinary ravens as their spies.
>
> Urwin Martikov (LG male human) is a [[Wereraven]] and a high-ranking member of the Keepers of the Feather. Urwin's wife and business partner, Danika Dorakova (LG female human), is also a [[Wereraven]], as are their two sons, Brom and Bray. They run the Blue Water Inn in [[The Town of Vallaki]], where another 1d4 wereravens are present at any given time.
>
> If the characters earn the trust of the wereravens in Vallaki, the Keepers of the Feather will watch their backs. The next time the characters get themselves in serious trouble, a group of 1d4 [[Wereraven]] can show up to rescue or otherwise help them.
>
> Baba Lysaga has recently uncovered the Keepers as a potential threat to Strahd, through her allies in Castle Ravenloft.
