The household and servants of [[Strahd von Zarovich]], lord of Barovia, whose castle looms over Barovia village from atop a great pillar of stone. His forces besieged the Burgomaster's manor, his wagon carries a crest the party didn't recognise, and he has promised that his "people" will watch for [[Arabelle]] and [[Krogarov]]. [[Doru]] went to the castle with the peasants' revolt and came back a vampire.
