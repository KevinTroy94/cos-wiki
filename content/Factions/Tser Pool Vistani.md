The [[Vistani]] of the camp near a tributary of the [[River Ivlis]], which the Senostrov brothers at [[The Gallows Oak]] pointed the party toward on their first night in Barovia. [[NPCS/Madam Eva|Madam Eva]] read the party's fortune here, and [[Laszlo, The Storyteller]] promised them safe passage beyond [[The Mists]] in exchange for bringing home [[Arabelle]], a girl missing from the camp.
