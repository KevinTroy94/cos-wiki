An order of knights, apparently the Baron's men, who carry the grinning gold sun on their surcoats. The party met one, [[Dmitry]], on the road to Vallaki, duelling [[Ernest Festibule]], whom he called "a fugitive and a vagabond". Festibule's crime, by his own telling, was against merriment, or some such. Their captain is [[Izek Strazni]].

In [[Vallaki]], a sign details the Order's methods of "discipline": "Contentment", "Amusement", "Convalescence" and "Dischargement". Some townsfolk wear strapped masks, called masks of contentment. Little more is known about them.
