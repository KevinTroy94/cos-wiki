An order of knights, apparently the Baron's men, who carry the grinning gold sun on their surcoats. The party met one, [[Dmitry]], on the road to Vallaki, duelling [[Ernest Festibule]], whom he called "a fugitive and a vagabond". Festibule's crime, by his own telling, was against merriment, or some such. Little more is known about them.
