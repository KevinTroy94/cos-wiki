The wolves of Barovia are larger and more cunning than those found elsewhere. They hunt in coordinated packs and show no fear of fire or armed groups. [[Virro]] caught a glimpse of one among the trees on the night the party entered the mists, a great wolf with eyes burning an unnatural amber, before it slipped back into the dark.

> [!dmspoiler]
> The wolves of Barovia serve Strahd directly and cannot be charmed or frightened. They act as his spies and enforcers throughout the valley, reporting to him at dawn and dusk alongside his other agents. Dire wolves, significantly larger and more dangerous, also roam the Svalich Woods and answer to the same master.
>
> It was dire wolves that killed [[Garlan Shepard]] on the night the party entered Barovia.
