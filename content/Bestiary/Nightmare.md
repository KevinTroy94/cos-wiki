[[Retnuh]] rides a nightmare named Buccephalus, given to him by [[Strahd von Zarovich]] on the road outside Barovia village. She is ash grey, her coat seeming to smoulder faintly at the edges, with a mane amber-bright and hot to the touch and eyes that burn a deep, steady orange. She moves with an unnatural, smooth grace, even on wet cobblestones.

[[Virro]] suspects she could be watching the party and reporting back to her old master. When he slapped her flank to drive her off, she reared and kicked him square in the chest, throwing him several feet and leaving him unconscious with his surcoat smouldering. [[Ireena Kolyana]] has since proposed using her as a channel to feed Strahd misinformation.

> [!dmspoiler]
> Buccephalus can act as a scrying focus that Strahd sees through.
>
> Nightmares are fiendish horses from the lower planes, bound to the will of powerful evil masters. They can travel the Ethereal Plane and carry their riders across it. They do not tire and do not fear. In Barovia, Nightmares serve Strahd and his most trusted servants. Bucephalus, Strahd's personal Nightmare, is the most feared of them.
>
> A Nightmare will not willingly serve a good-aligned rider. If forced to, it attempts to unseat or harm them at every opportunity.
