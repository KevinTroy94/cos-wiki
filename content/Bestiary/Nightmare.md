> [!dmspoiler]
> Nightmares are fiendish horses from the lower planes, bound to the will of powerful evil masters. They can travel the Ethereal Plane and carry their riders across it. They do not tire and do not fear. In Barovia, Nightmares serve Strahd and his most trusted servants. Bucephalus, Strahd's personal Nightmare, is the most feared of them.
>
> A Nightmare will not willingly serve a good-aligned rider. If forced to, it attempts to unseat or harm them at every opportunity.
