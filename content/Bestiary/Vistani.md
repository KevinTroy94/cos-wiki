Wandering people who travel Barovia in barrel-topped wagons, moving freely through the mists that trap everyone else. Flamboyant where Barovians are drab, they dress in bright colours, laugh often, and drink heartily. They are silversmiths, coppersmiths, storytellers, fortune tellers, and horse traders.

Vistani consider it a terrible sin to take valuables from a corpse, though they consider it fair game to steal from the living.

**Encounters**

- [[Session 1 - into the mists|Session 1]]: a camp near a tributary of the [[River Ivlis]], where the party met [[Madam Eva]], [[Laszlo, The Storyteller]], [[Norella]], and others.
- [[Session 2 - A Sad village of few souls|Session 2]]: three Vistani women, [[Mirabelle]], [[Sorvia]], and [[Alenka]], at [[The Blood of the Vine]] tavern in Barovia village, which it emerged they own.

> [!dmspoiler]
> The Vistani are free to come and go from Barovia because of an ancient debt Strahd owes them. Centuries ago, Vistani tended his wounds after a battle and returned him safely home without asking for payment. He has honoured that debt ever since.
>
> Many Vistani have since allowed themselves to be corrupted by Strahd and consider him their king. These serve as his eyes and ears beyond the mists, luring adventurers into his domain and reporting back at dawn and dusk. They will lie to protect him. The potions they sell claiming to allow escape from Barovia are fake.
>
> Not all Vistani serve Strahd willingly. Some resist him quietly. Telling the two apart requires careful observation.
