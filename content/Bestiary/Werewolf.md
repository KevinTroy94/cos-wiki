Humanoids cursed with the ability to transform into wolves or a terrifying hybrid of man and beast. In human form they are indistinguishable from ordinary people. The party encountered evidence of werewolf activity in the woods outside Barovia, where [[Brinn]] and his companions were attacked by wolf creatures leaving bite marks consistent with something far larger and more deliberate than a common wolf.

> [!dmspoiler]
> Werewolves in human form often pose as trappers or hunters. They test strangers for silvered weapons before deciding whether to attack. In wolf form they shadow their prey for hours, waiting for the right moment to strike.
>
> A werewolf can only be reliably harmed by silver or magic weapons. Their lair is a cave complex overlooking Lake Baratok. They keep prisoners there.
>
> The werewolves of Barovia answer to a pack leader named Kiril Stoyanovich, a brutal man who has fully embraced the curse. Not all members of the pack share his enthusiasm.
