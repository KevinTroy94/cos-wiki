The common people of Barovia. Pale, hollow-eyed, and worn down by centuries of fear. They stare openly at strangers and rarely speak unless spoken to, and even then offer little. Most keep to the three settlements, unwilling to risk the roads or the woods after dark.

They trade in old coins bearing the profile of [[Strahd von Zarovich]] and dress plainly, keeping whatever valuables they have hidden indoors. They are not unfriendly by nature, but trust is hard won here. Those who do warm to outsiders tend to do so with a quiet, lasting loyalty.

> [!dmspoiler]
> Not all Barovians have souls. Roughly one in ten do. The rest are empty shells conjured by Strahd's consciousness to populate his domain. Soulless Barovians experience fear but never laugh or cry. They have no spark, no charm, no imagination. They dress in drab colours and are more compliant and depressed than those with souls.
>
> Strahd feeds on the blood of Barovians with souls and can tell at a glance which are which. Soulless Barovians cease to exist the moment they leave the valley.
>
> Barovians with souls reincarnate within the domain when they die, their souls cycling through generations. This is why Ireena Kolyana looks exactly like Strahd's lost love Tatyana. They share the same soul.
