Harengon Fighter

## Background

Though his memories are blurred, Virro was born in the Feywild, his clan bound to the service of a minor fey lord as warriors. He took to combat naturally from a young age and developed a strong fondness for it. But the harsh discipline of his teachers and the strict, frugal life under his lord's command bred a deep resentment in him over the years.

His way out came when a fairy gate to the material plane appeared. His lord and a rival fey both moved to claim it, and in the skirmish that followed, Virro saw his chance. One of his own comrades realised what he intended and moved to block him. He doesn't remember much about them. He didn't hesitate. One strike, and his escape was secured.

He ran as far from the gate as he could. After a few days wandering, his memories of the Feywild growing foggier with each mile, he stumbled across a trade caravan under attack from goblins. One of the goblins took him for an enemy and attacked, and that settled the question of whose side he was on. With the goblins defeated, the caravan took him on for the rest of the journey and paid him the wage of a guard who hadn't survived.

That night he spent nearly every coin on food, drink, gambling, and music. He found a drinking companion in a travelling scholar who filled the hours with tales of trolls, dungeons, and adventurers.

He woke the next morning in a field on the edge of town, head pounding, stomach turning. He dunked his head in a nearby stream and when he lifted it, he looked out over rolling hills, lush forests, and distant mountains. He knew then that he was free. Free to choose his own fights, spend his rewards how he liked, and go wherever he wanted. Endless horizons, and he intended to seize everything he could.

## Session 1 - Into the Mists

Virro was among the five travellers in [[Garlan Shepard]]'s wagon when the fog rolled in and swallowed the road. He was the one who caught the silhouette of a great wolf among the trees, its eyes burning an unnatural amber, before it slipped back into the brush. When Garlan collapsed and the wagon broke apart, the party fled on foot.

At the [[The Gallows Oak]] inn, Virro sat with [[Adeline]], who agreed to add him to the tapestry she was embroidering, a long line of adventurers stitched hand in hand, each with an X across their eyes. When she finished his caricature, the tapestry slid from the table, the figures seeming to drag him down with them.

At the Vistani camp, Virro had his fortune read by [[Madam Eva]] along with the rest of the party.

## Session 2 - A Sad Village of Few Souls

Restless after the reading, Virro slipped outside to find [[Laszlo, The Storyteller]], who he'd clocked earlier. When he asked if there was anyone worth crossing blades with, Laszlo's grin widened. A crowd gathered quickly, money changed hands, and Laszlo played to every parry for a laugh. Midway through the bout he scratched a magic circle into the dirt beneath their feet, meant to snare Virro mid-step. It didn't work. Virro broke through his guard and put him on the ground. The party collected 18 gold, with [[Edric Storm]] the only one who had bet on him to win.

In the [[The Blood of the Vine]] tavern in Barovia village, Virro pressed [[Arik]] for information about [[Arabelle]] and got nothing back. He had better luck with the three Vistani women at the table, [[Mirabelle]], [[Sorvia]], and [[Alenka]], though Mirabelle refused to answer questions straight and turned it into a drinking game instead. Virro played along.

Later, he attempted to alter the tab sheet while Arik had stepped away from the bar. Mirabelle caught him, told him it was brazen, wrote his name on the tabs, and threw them in the fire.

He and [[Edric Storm]] went together to the Burgomaster's manor, finding it besieged. [[Ireena Kolyana]] met them at the door and took them for agents of the dark lord. Once they convinced her otherwise, she let them inside, where the body of [[Kolyan Indirovich]] lay in state.
