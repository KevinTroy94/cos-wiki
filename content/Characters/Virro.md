Though my memories are blurred, I'm sure I was born in the Feywild, my clan bound to the service of a minor fey lord to serve as warriors. Prepared from a young age, I took to the arts of combat naturally and developed a strong fondness for them. However, the harsh discipline from my teachers and lord and the strict and frugal life afforded led me to resent those commanding me. This went on for many years before a way out appeared. A fairy gate to the material plane had appeared, and both my lord and another rival fey took interest in it. During a skirmish for the gate, I took my chance to escape. One of my own comrades, realising what I intended, tried to block me. I can't remember much about them, but I know I did not hesitate; they stood in my way, one strike, and my escape was secured.
Finding myself in the unfamiliar material plane, I simply ran as far as I could from the fairy gate before anyone could follow. After a few days of wandering and my memories of the Feywild becoming foggier, I happened upon a trade caravan battling with goblins. One such goblin took me for a foe and attacked, and so I found my side in the battle decided for me. With the goblins defeated, the caravan thanked me, and I joined them on their journey. Upon reaching our destination, I was paid the wage of one caravan guard who had not survived the goblins and parted ways with the caravan. That night, I used near every coin I was given on food & drink, gambling, and music. I also found a drinking buddy in a travelling scholar who regaled me with tales of trolls, dungeons, adventurers, and other epic stories.
The following morning, I awoke in a field on the edge of the town, my head pounding and my stomach queasy. Stumbling to a small stream, I dunked my head into the water for some reprieve. When I lifted my head from the stream, I gazed upon rolling hills, lush forests and distant mountains. I knew in that moment that I was free to choose my own fights, spend my rewards how I wanted and travel where I so wished. Endless horizons stretched before me, and I was going to seize everything I could.
