## Session 1 - Into the Mists

Retnuh was among the five travellers in [[Garlan Shepard]]'s wagon when the fog rolled in. He sat up on the driver's box beside Garlan and was the first to notice the old man looked unwell, four days of cold rain having left him fevered. When Garlan collapsed and the wagon fell apart, Retnuh fled with the rest of the party down the Old Svalich Road.

He was present at the [[The Gallows Oak]] inn and at the Vistani camp, where [[Madam Eva]] read the party's fortune.

## Session 2 - A Sad Village of Few Souls

In the [[The Blood of the Vine]] tavern, Retnuh found himself talking more and more with [[Sorvia]] as the night wore on, something easy and mutual sparking between them. When she offered to show him her room upstairs, the two retired together.

Lying upstairs afterward, Sorvia let slip that [[Alenka]] had seen [[Arabelle]] and [[Krogarov]] recently in town, with a wagon and two heavy sacks. Their conversation was cut short when a vision of spectral blue light appeared in the street below. Retnuh rushed out to find spirits in the form of knights, adventurers, sorcerers, and townsfolk marching in a loose, silent formation through the fog. He planted himself in their path to stop them. Several passed straight through him. He followed.

## Session 3 - Spawn

Retnuh followed the ghostly procession out of the village and onto the road through the rain, until it passed straight through a black lacquered wagon standing driverless at the roadside. Harnessed to it was an ash grey horse with a smouldering coat and burning orange eyes. He fed her a handful of nuts, which roasted lightly in his palm as she ate.

A voice behind him admired the horse, and the two talked for a long while without Retnuh ever turning around. The stranger, tall and pale in fine, old-fashioned clothes, told him of the curse on the land and of the mists that unravel a person's memory. Retnuh found he was already struggling to recall much of his life before Barovia. The stranger invited him to dine at Castle Ravenloft to see what could be recovered, promised to have his people watch for [[Arabelle]] and [[Krogarov]], and let him ride the horse, Buccephalus, for as long as she chooses to stay. In return, he asked Retnuh to bring [[Ireena Kolyana]] to the castle. Retnuh agreed, once Arabelle is found. The stranger was [[Strahd von Zarovich]].

Retnuh rode Buccephalus back into the village and found the rest of the party at the centre of town, grinning as he explained where the horse had come from. The mood soured at Strahd's name. When [[Virro]] called the horse a spy, Retnuh insisted, "She is not a spy. She is a good horse." Virro slapped her flank to drive her off and was kicked unconscious for it, and Retnuh muttered that he deserved it.

He urged the party to come with him to Castle Ravenloft, saying Strahd could help them with the curse and the mists. Ireena refused.
