Mountain Dwarf Paladin

## Background

Once based in the north ward of Waterdeep, a former paladin of Ilmater and member of the Order of the Golden Cups. Once, coming across an injured boy, Ignacious asked how he was hurt. The boy said he fell. He healed him.

He would cross paths with the boy a few more times. Each time, the boy said he had fallen. The injuries were clearly inflicted by another. Ignacious asked if his father hurt him. The boy told him everything.

He confronted the father, who was apologetic enough, or too much a coward to raise his voice to a man in mail. Ignacious left him with a warning.

Three days later, tending to the sick near St. Lauren Hospice, a young girl came running up to him, injured and crying. Before he could speak she said the boy's name. They were fighting.

He ran.

When he had warned the father not to harm his son, the man had turned to beating his daughter instead. The older brother tried to stop it and eventually struck his father. The father took this as justification to hit back.

When Ignacious found the boy, he was already gone.

He was in plain garb that day, no mail or shield. The father didn't recognise him. He had no weapon, but he'd cut bandages that morning and still had the knife on him.

He stabbed the father in the gut. The man fell and groaned.

"Don't worry, I'll patch you right up," he said through a clenched jaw.

And he did. His healing hands worked just fine. So he stabbed him again. This continued long after his hands could heal anymore.

His fellow Cupbearers found him before the city guard did. Hands slick with blood, a grim corpse at his feet, a traumatised girl with no family left in the corner.

He was cast out. Even the Companions of the Noble Heart thought he had gone too far.

Now he wanders, his oath and spirit as broken as the god he once served.

## Session 1 - Into the Mists

Ignacious was among the five travellers picked up by [[Garlan Shepard]] on the road north of Neverwinter, alongside [[Virro]], [[Edric Storm]], [[Retnuh]], and [[Red]]. When the fog swallowed the road and Garlan's fever worsened, Ignacious moved to heal him but was too late. The old man collapsed from the driver's box and was dragged into the treeline by something unseen.

The party fled on foot, eventually reaching the [[The Gallows Oak]] inn, where Ignacious helped negotiate lodging in exchange for labour. He sat with the Senostrov family and learned of the land they had wandered into. At the Vistani camp near the [[River Ivlis]], he entered [[Madam Eva]]'s tent with the rest of the party and had his fortune read.

## Session 2 - A Sad Village of Few Souls

In Barovia village, Ignacious took it upon himself to go door to door searching for [[Arabelle]]. He found his way to [[Bildrath's Mercantile]], where [[Bildrath Cantomere]] was standoffish but eventually let slip that [[Krogarov]] had passed through with a wagon in need of repair and a horse to be re-shoed, helped by Bildrath's [[boy]] and leaving with three large sacks.

Earlier in the evening, stepping outside the [[The Blood of the Vine]] for air, he was approached by an old woman pushing a wooden cart through the fog. She offered him a soft brown pastry and spoke of her sisters having moved on, leaving her alone at an old mill outside the village. He accepted the pastry. The moment he swallowed it, the world sharpened and softened all at once, colours deepening, sounds arriving late and layered. He sat with it washing over him.

Continuing his search, he found a townhouse with an open door and caught sight of someone being dragged inside. He entered. The door closed behind him.
