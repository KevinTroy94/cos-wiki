Mountain Dwarf Palidin
Once based in the north ward of Waterdeep, a former paladin of Ilmater and member of Order of the Golden Cups. Once, I came coming across an injured boy. I asked how he was hurt, he said he fell, and healed him.
I would cross paths with this boy on a few more occasions, each time he said he had fallen. The injuries were clearly inflicted my another, someone bigger than him. I asked the boy if his father hurt him. The boy told me everything.
I confronted the boys father, he was very apologetic (or too much a coward to raise voice or hand to a man in maile) so I left him with a warning.
3 days later, while I tended to the sick near St. Lauren Hospice, a young girl came running up to be, she was injured and crying, before I could say anything she said the boy's name that they were fighting.
I ran.
When I warned the father not to harm his son anymore he took to beating his daughter instead, the older brother tried to stop this eventually struck his father.
The father took this to mean he was justified to hit back.
When I found the boy he was laying on the ground, I tried my healing hands but he was already gone.
I was in my plain garb, no maile or shield, when the father saw me he didn't recognise me.
I had no weapon, but earlier that morn I had to cut some bandages, I had used a knife which I still had.
I stabbed him in the gut, he fell, he groaned.
"don't worry I'll patch you right up" I said through a clenched jaw.
And I did just that. My healing hands worked on him just fine.
So I stabbed him again.
This continued long after my hands could heal anymore.
My fellow Cup bearers found me before the city guard. My hands slick with blood, a grim corpse at my foot, and a traumatised girl with no family remaining in the corner.
I was cast out, even the Companions of the Noble Heart thought I had gone too far.
Now I wander, my oath and spirit as broken as the god I once served.
