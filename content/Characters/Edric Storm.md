## Session 1 - Into the Mists

Edric was among the five travellers in [[Garlan Shepard]]'s wagon when the fog rolled in. When the party climbed down to investigate the woods, he cast a light spell on the wagon's cover to illuminate the area, and it was this glow that let [[Virro]] spot the silhouette of a great wolf among the trees, its eyes burning an unnatural amber.

When the wagon broke apart and the party fled, Edric managed to get control of the panicked ponies [[Keneticut]] and [[Stanford]] for a time before Stanford broke free. He was present for [[Madam Eva]]'s reading at the Vistani camp.

## Session 2 - A Sad Village of Few Souls

Outside the [[The Blood of the Vine]] tavern, Edric heard raised voices and stepped out to find [[Morgantha]] standing before a pair of hollow-eyed Barovians, cradling their swaddled child. When he and [[Virro]] couldn't reason her into giving the child back, Edric offered her a favour, any favour, whenever she chose to collect it. Morgantha accepted without hesitation and handed the child over, looking far too pleased about the arrangement.

He and Virro later went together to the Burgomaster's manor, finding it under siege. [[Ireena Kolyana]] met them at the door, suspicious they were agents of the dark lord. Once convinced otherwise, she let them inside, where the body of [[Kolyan Indirovich]] lay in state.
