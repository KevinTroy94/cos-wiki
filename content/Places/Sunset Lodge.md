A small whitewashed cabin about an hour's walk north of [[Vallaki]], standing alone in a clearing of birch trees where the forest goes unnaturally quiet. A sun is painted over the door. A tunnel behind the wall of the undercroft of [[St. Andral's Church]] leads out to it.

According to [[Bozena]], the lodge is one of the punishments meted out in Vallaki. The first family sent out to it, three years ago, left Kozlov Manor standing empty in town.

On the porch, a row of boots and slippers waits beside the entrance, neatly ordered heel to heel, cleaned and cared for. The porch is dusted with a fine pale grit, and four statues of crouched creatures with smiling, sun-faced heads sit on the roof. They are [[Sunset Gargoyle|Sunset Gargoyles]].

Inside is a single neatly kept room, spotless and tightly made, with a guest book on the table and a shelf of mementos from former guests, among them a pocket watch engraved **P. Hask** ([[Pieter Hask]]). The rug at the centre of the room is rich with wine-dark reds and a border of faded gold, and far too clean for a room otherwise coated in dust. It is an [[Animated Rug of Smothering]].
