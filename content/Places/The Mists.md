A thick fog that rolled in over the hills of Neverwinter Wood and swallowed the road beneath [[Garlan Shepard]]'s wagon. As it thickened, the trees became ghosts in the haze and the road vanished entirely. When it thinned, the party was somewhere else: Barovia.

The mists hang over everything here. The Senostrov brothers at [[The Gallows Oak]] are wary of what lurks in them. The [[Vistani]] seem to move through them freely, and [[Laszlo, The Storyteller]] has offered the party safe passage beyond them in exchange for bringing [[Arabelle]] home.

On the road outside Barovia village, [[Strahd von Zarovich]] told [[Retnuh]] that the mists have a way of unravelling a person's memory the longer they linger in Barovia. Retnuh found he was already struggling to recall much of his life from before.

> [!dmspoiler]
> A deadly fog surrounds the land of Barovia and engulfs any creature that tries to leave. Even flying creatures are subject to the fog's effects, which are as follows:
> - A creature that starts its turn in the fog must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution saving throw or gain one level of exhaustion. This exhaustion can't be removed while the creature is in the fog.
> - No matter how far a creature travels in the fog, or in which direction it goes, it gets turned around so that it eventually finds itself back in Barovia.
> - The area within the fog is heavily obscured.
>
