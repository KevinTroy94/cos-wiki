The only general goods store in Barovia village. Run by [[Bildrath Cantomere]], a standoffish man with little patience for questions and even less for haggling. His [[boy]], works the stock and handles anything that requires lifting.

The shelves are sparse but functional. Bildrath stocks what the village needs to survive and charges accordingly, with no interest in negotiation. Coin bearing [[Strahd von Zarovich]]'s profile is the only currency that means anything here.
