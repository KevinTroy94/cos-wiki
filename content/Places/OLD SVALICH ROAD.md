The road the party followed into Barovia after fleeing the wreck of [[Garlan Shepard]]'s wagon. At a fork, a sign pointed one way to [[The Last Gasp]] and the other to the Old Svalich Road. [[Red]] went up toward the Last Gasp. The rest of the party took the Old Svalich Road, and after some time reached [[The Gallows Oak]].
