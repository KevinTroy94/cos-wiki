An inn on the [[OLD SVALICH ROAD]], run by the brothers [[Oskar Senostrov]] and [[Arjun Senostrov]] and their adopted daughter [[Adeline]]. It was the first shelter the party found after fleeing the wreck of [[Garlan Shepard]]'s wagon.

The brothers agreed to put the party up for the night in exchange for some labour. It was here that the party learned their coins were of no use in Barovia, where most currency bears the profile of [[Strahd von Zarovich]], lord of the land. The Senostrovs have no quarrel with the Count. They are wary of what lurks in the mists, but not panicked by it. They pointed the party toward a camp of [[Vistani]] near a tributary of the [[River Ivlis]].

Adeline is embroidering a large tapestry to line one wall of the inn: a long procession of travellers who have passed through, stitched hand in hand, each with an X across their eyes. She added [[Virro]] to it. When she finished his caricature, the tapestry slid from the table, the figures seeming to drag him down with them.
