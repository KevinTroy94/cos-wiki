The church of the Morninglord in [[Vallaki]], tended by [[Father Lucian]]. The church's sexton petitioned the Baron about stolen church property and was dismissed with "All will be well."

The stolen property is the bones of St. Andral, which lay in the church's crypt. According to Father Lucian, without them Vallaki's protection against the undead won't function. He has asked [[Ignacious Teegan|Ignacious]] and [[Edric Storm|Edric]] to help find them.

Beneath the altar is a hidden undercroft. Turning a crank on the pipe organ set into the wall behind the altar and playing a note on the keys releases the altar, which slides aside on rails to reveal a stair down. There, a glass cloche dome over bare satin held the bones of Saint Andral, who died to keep the land that became Vallaki safe from an overwhelming force. The case stands open and empty. A decoy grave for the saint in the cemetery was dug up the night before the party arrived.

Behind a section of the undercroft wall, [[Virro]] found the thieves' way in: a tunnel that runs out beyond the town's bounds to [[Sunset Lodge]].
