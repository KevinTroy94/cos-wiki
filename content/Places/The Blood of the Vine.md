A tavern on the main square of Barovia village. In the fog, a single shaft of light cuts through the haze like a solid pillar, the only real brightness in the square, and above the gaping doorway a sign swings crooked on its chains. Inside, warmth and firelight replace the cold, though the atmosphere is no less heavy.

The barman is [[Arik]], who gives nothing away and only asks whether you want a drink. The tavern is owned by three Vistani women, [[Mirabelle]], [[Sorvia]], and [[Alenka]], who sit at a table near the front and see that tabs get paid. Sorvia keeps a room upstairs.

The party spent their first night in the village here, drinking pitchers of wine and playing Mirabelle's game of Truths. [[Ignacious Teegan]] bought a drink for [[Ismark The Lesser]], who joined the table until a question about his sister drove him out into the street. [[Virro]] tried to alter his tab while Arik was away, and Mirabelle burned it instead. Outside the door, [[Morgantha]] sold her pastries and bargained with [[Edric Storm]] over a Barovian child.
