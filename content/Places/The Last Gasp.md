A place up the other branch of the fork at the start of the [[OLD SVALICH ROAD]]. The party has never been there. [[Red]] split from the group at the fork and headed toward it.

When the party met Red again, he said a lady in white at the Last Gasp had asked him to pick a weapon. He couldn't choose one, so she gave him a lilac book instead. After he died and rose again, he set off into the mists to find her, hoping she could fix him.
