# Vallaki Notice Board

The notice board outside the Blue Water Inn is covered in announcements for the Festival of the Blazing Sun.

> [!dmspoiler]
> The festival is a sham. Baron Vallakovich knows morale is collapsing and uses the festivals to maintain control.
> See [[Baron Vallakovich's Private Journal]] for his true thoughts.

Travellers passing through Vallaki will notice the guards are unusually tense.

> [!dmspoiler] Guard Patrol Schedule
> Guards rotate every 4 hours. The north gate is unwatched between 2am and 3am.
> Izek personally patrols the market square at midnight.

The inn itself is a two-storey building with a carved blue heron above the door.
