# Marta the Innkeeper

Marta runs the Silver Coin inn on the edge of Vallaki. She is warm to travellers and always has a hot meal ready.

She has lived in Vallaki her whole life and knows most of the locals by name.

> [!dmspoiler] DM Only — Marta's Secret
> Marta is secretly a spy for [[Lady Wachter]]. She reports on any strangers who ask too many questions.
> She also knows the location of [[The Hidden Cellar]], a note that should never be published.
> Her contact is [[Dmitri Krezkov]] who visits every new moon.

Players can ask Marta about local rumours. She will mention the Festival of the Blazing Sun with a nervous laugh.
